Dr. Lu joined the group as a postdoctoral researcher in August 2025. He received his B.S. and Ph.D. from Peking University. His doctoral work in Lin Zhang's group focused on the complex, nonlinear relationship between anthropogenic emissions and surface PM2.5 concentrations, aiming to identify cost-effective air pollution mitigation pathways. His research interests span inverse modeling, PM2.5 chemistry, and cost-effectiveness analysis.
